# GradTrack — Product Goal

Our goal is to develop GradTrack, a student-centered graduation project management platform that helps university teams organize their work, assign and track tasks, manage project milestones and deadlines, and collaborate efficiently throughout the graduation project lifecycle.

The platform will allow students to submit files and links for milestones, receive supervisor feedback, and track approval status. Assigned supervisors will be able to review their teams' progress and approve or return submissions, while programme coordinators will have visibility into overall project progress.

Our initial goal is to deliver a functional, tested MVP that supports team-supervisor assignments, milestone tracking, submissions, and supervisor reviews, while automatically flagging late submissions and enforcing approval permissions.
